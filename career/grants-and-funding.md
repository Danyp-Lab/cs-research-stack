# 💰 Grants, Fellowships & Research Funding

> External funding is the lifeblood of academic research. For PhD students, competitive fellowships enhance your stipend, boost your CV, and — crucially — signal to future employers that external experts validated your research potential.

---

## 1. PhD Fellowships (United States)

### NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (NSF GRFP)

The most prestigious US PhD fellowship for STEM students.

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Amount** | $37,000/year stipend + $16,000/year tuition allowance for 3 years |
| **Eligibility** | US citizens/permanent residents; apply in years 1–2 of PhD or in final year of undergrad |
| **Selection rate** | ~15–20% of applicants |
| **Deadline** | Mid-October annually |

**Application components:**
- Personal Statement (3 pages)
- Research Statement (2 pages)  
- 3 reference letters

> [!TIP]
> The NSF GRFP evaluates on two criteria: **Intellectual Merit** (scientific contribution) and **Broader Impacts** (societal benefit). Your statements must explicitly address both. Many strong applications fail by treating Broader Impacts as an afterthought.

**Resources:**
* [NSF GRFP Website](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/)
* [Alex Lang's GRFP Guide](https://www.alexhunterlang.com/nsf-fellowship) — Detailed advice + example essays
* [Mallory Ladd's GRFP Examples](http://www.malloryladd.com/nsf-grfp-advice.html) — Funded and reviewed applications

### NDSEG Fellowship (National Defense Science and Engineering)

Department of Defense fellowship with emphasis on defense-relevant research.

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Amount** | ~$38,400/year stipend for 3 years |
| **Eligibility** | US citizens applying to or in early PhD |
| **Areas** | CS, EE, materials, mathematics, physics, chemistry |
| **Deadline** | December annually |

### DOE CSGF (Department of Energy Computational Science)

Prestigious fellowship focused on HPC and computational science.

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Amount** | $45,000/year + tuition + $1,000 academic allowance for up to 4 years |
| **Requirement** | 12-week practicum at a DOE national laboratory |
| **Deadline** | January annually |

## 3. Internal University Funding

Before pursuing external grants, exhaust internal sources — they have higher acceptance rates:

- **Department travel grants** — For conference attendance
- **Graduate school fellowship competitions** — Often overlooked
- **Center/institute seed grants** — Cross-department research centers often have small grants
- **Gift funds** — Industry-donated funds administered by advisors
- **Teaching assistant (TA) supplement** — Some TA positions include additional research funding

Ask your department's graduate coordinator or financial administrator what is available.

## 5. Conference Travel Funding

Don't pay for conference travel out of pocket. Multiple sources exist:

### Student Travel Grants

Most major CS conferences offer student travel grants:
- **NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR** — ~$500–2,000 for accepted paper authors
- **ACM Conferences (SIGCOMM, SOSP, etc.)** — SIGCOMM Student Research Competition grants
- **IEEE Conferences** — IEEE Young Professional travel awards

### Professional Society Grants

* **[ACM Student Travel Grants](https://www.acm.org/education/students)** — For ACM conference attendance
* **[IEEE Education Travel Grants](https://www.ieee.org/education_careers/education/index.html)**
* **[NSF Conference Travel Grants](https://www.nsf.gov/)** — Specific programs per conference

## 6. Grant Writing Tips

Grant writing is a learnable skill, and it compounds over a career:

### Core Principles

1. **Lead with the problem, not the solution** — Reviewers must be convinced the problem matters before they care about your approach.
2. **Be explicit about novelty** — State clearly what has not been done before, and why existing approaches fail.
3. **Make evaluation criteria concrete** — How will you know if the project succeeded?
4. **Broader impacts require genuine thought** — How does this change the world outside your lab?

### Resources for Grant Writing

* **[NSF Grant Proposal Guide](https://www.nsf.gov/publications/pub_summ.jsp?ods_key=pappg)** — Official guide (read the whole thing)
* **[Grant Forward](https://www.grantforward.com/)** — Database of funding opportunities across agencies
* **[PIVOT (ProQuest)](https://pivot.proquest.com/)** — Comprehensive funding database (institutional access)
* Your university's **Office of Sponsored Research** — They have grant writers and pre-submission review
