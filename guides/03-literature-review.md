# Literature Review Guide

*Get the most out of your literature mining and keep it reproducible.*

## Key Steps
- Define research questions and keywords
- Use semantic‑search tools (Connected Papers, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex)
- Organize PDFs with Zotero / Mendeley and tag them
- Summarize each paper in a one‑pager (question, method, result, relevance)
- Build a citation map (graph) to spot gaps

> Tip: Export your Zotero library to a CSV and feed it into a mind‑map tool.
