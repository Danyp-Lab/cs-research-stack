# Research Proposal Guide

*This guide walks you through the essential components of a research proposal, from problem statement to budget planning.*

## Sections
- **Motivation & Problem Statement**
- **Literature Gap Identification**
- **Methodology Overview**
- **Work Plan & Timeline**
- **Resources & Budget**
- **Impact & Outcomes**

> Tip: Keep the proposal to ~5 pages for MSc and ~10 pages for PhD.
