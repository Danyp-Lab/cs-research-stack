## 📋 Quick Application Checklist (Universal)

| ✅ Item | What to Submit |
|---|---|
| **Transcripts** | Official undergraduate (and prior master’s) records |
| **Standardized Tests** | GRE (US), TOEFL/IELTS for non‑English speakers |
| **English Proficiency** | IELTS ≥ 6.5 / TOEFL ≥ 90 |
| **CV** | One‑page (MSc) or two‑page (PhD) highlighting research, projects, publications |
| **SOP / Research Statement** | Tailored to each university’s research groups |
| **Letters of Recommendation** | 2–3 from professors/supervisors who know your research potential |
| **Portfolio** | GitHub repos, demo videos, arXiv papers, etc. |
| **Proof of Funding** | Required only for self‑funded MSc (bank statements, scholarship award) |
| **Application Form & Fee** | Online portal specific to each university |
| **Passport Copy** | Needed for visa processing after acceptance |

## 🗓️ Suggested Timeline (12 Months)

| Month | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **-12 to -9** | Identify target region(s), shortlist 8‑12 programs, read faculty pages |
| **-9 to -6** | Prepare GRE/TOEFL, request transcripts, begin SOP drafts |
| **-6 to -4** | Contact potential supervisors (short email with CV & research idea) |
| **-4 to -2** | Finalize SOP, gather recommendation letters, assemble portfolio |
| **-2 to -1** | Submit applications (most deadlines Dec‑Jan) |
| **0** | Interviews (if any) and wait for decisions |
| **+1 to +3** | Accept offer, apply for visa, arrange housing |

## 🛠️ Helpful Tools

- **Overleaf** – collaborative LaTeX for SOPs and research statements.
- **Zotero / Mendeley** – manage citations for research statements.
- **Google Scholar Alerts** – follow work of prospective supervisors.
- **ORCID** – persistent ID for publications and grants.
- **GitHub** – showcase code and link to specific commits in your CV.
- **Canva** – design a clean, ATS‑friendly CV (export PDF).
