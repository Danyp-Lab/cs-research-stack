# Publishing Guide

*Navigate the scholarly publishing landscape efficiently and increase the impact of your work.*

## 1. Choosing the Right Venue
- **Scope match** – read the journal’s aims & scope; ensure your contribution fits.
- **Impact metrics** – consider Impact Factor, h‑index, and community reputation.
- **Open‑access policy** – gold OA (author pays), green OA (self‑archive), or hybrid.
- **Speed** – typical review time (look for median days on journal website).
- **Conference vs. Journal** – CS often values top conferences (e.g., NeurIPS, PLDI) for fast dissemination.

## 2. Pre‑print and Early Dissemination
- Upload to **arXiv**, **arXiv‑CS**, **bioRxiv** (for interdisciplinary work) before submission.
- Include a clear statement in the manuscript: *"This work was posted on arXiv (arXiv:xxxx.xxxxx) on DATE."*
- Verify the venue’s policy on pre‑prints (most CS conferences allow it).

## 3. Manuscript Preparation Checklist
| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Template** | Use the official LaTeX/Word template provided by the venue. |
| **Length** | Respect page limits (including references). |
| **Figures** | High‑resolution (300 dpi), readable fonts, captions with concise description. |
| **Code & Data** | Provide a URL (GitHub, Zenodo) and a DOI in the *Data Availability* section. |
| **Ethics Statement** | Include if required (e.g., for ML with human data). |
| **Supplementary Material** | Upload additional experiments or proofs as separate PDF/ZIP. |

## 4. Open‑Access Options
- **Gold OA**: Pay‑to‑publish (Springer Open, IEEE Access). Check for institutional discounts.
- **Green OA**: Self‑archive the accepted manuscript in your institutional repository after the embargo period.
- **Diamond OA**: No APC, often community‑run journals (e.g., JMLR). Great for CS.

## 5. Post‑Publication Promotion
- Share the DOI on **Twitter/X**, **LinkedIn**, and relevant Discord channels.
- Add the paper to **Google Scholar**, **Semantic Scholar**, and **ResearchGate** profiles.
- Deposit code and datasets with a permanent DOI (Zenodo integration with GitHub).

## 6. Checklist
- [ ] Identify 3–5 suitable venues and compare metrics.
- [ ] Prepare a pre‑print and upload to arXiv.
- [ ] Draft manuscript using the official template.
- [ ] Ensure ethical statements and reproducibility statements are included.
- [ ] Select open‑access route and plan APC funding if needed.
- [ ] Submit and track the manuscript via the journal’s portal.
- [ ] After acceptance, update repository with DOI and share broadly.
