# Conference Preparation Guide

*Make the most of conferences – from winning abstract acceptance to delivering an impactful talk.*

## 1. Selecting the Right Conference
- **Scope alignment** – ensure your work fits the conference’s topics.
- **Reputation** – consider acceptance rate, citation impact, and community presence.
- **Location & Timing** – factor in travel cost, visa requirements, and personal schedule.
- **Submission deadlines** – mark abstract, full‑paper, and camera‑ready dates.

## 2. Abstract & Paper Submission
| Step | Tips |
|---|---|
| **Read the Call for Papers (CFP)** | Highlight keywords, page limits, and formatting guidelines. |
| **Draft a concise abstract** | 150‑250 words: problem, method, main result, significance. |
| **Prepare the manuscript** | Use the official LaTeX/Word template; include a reproducibility statement. |
| **Check for plagiarism** | Run a similarity check (iThenticate, Turnitin) before submission. |
| **Submit early** | Avoid last‑minute technical issues on the portal. |

## 3. Funding & Travel Budget
- **Conference travel grants** – many venues (e.g., ACM SIGCOMM, NeurIPS) offer student travel awards.
- **Institutional funds** – apply to your department’s research travel pool.
- **Budget template** (example in USD):
  - Registration: $300–$800
  - Flight: $600–$1200
  - Accommodation: $100/night (3‑5 nights)
  - Meals & local transport: $150
  - Misc (visa, insurance): $200
- **Submit a budget justification** with your travel grant application.

## 4. Preparing the Presentation
### Poster
- **Layout** – Title at top, three columns, clear visuals, minimal text.
- **Fonts** – Minimum 24 pt for body, 36 pt for title.
- **QR code** – Link to paper or code repository.
- **Practice** – 2‑minute pitch for walkthrough.
### Talk (oral)
- **Timing** – Usually 12‑15 min; allocate 2 min for intro, 10 min for content, 2 min for Q&A.
- **Slides** – 1‑2 slides per minute, large fonts, high‑contrast colors.
- **Storytelling** – Problem → Approach → Results → Impact.
- **Rehearsal** – Record yourself, time, and refine.

## 5. Networking & Visibility
- **Pre‑conference** – Reach out to authors whose work you admire via email or Twitter.
- **During conference** – Attend social events, poster sessions, and workshops.
- **Post‑conference** – Upload a summary blog, share slides on Slideshare or Figshare, and add the proceedings to your CV.

## 6. Checklist
- [ ] Identify 2‑3 target conferences and note CFP dates.
- [ ] Draft abstract and get feedback from advisor.
- [ ] Prepare manuscript according to template.
- [ ] Apply for travel funding (grant, department, external).
- [ ] Create poster/talk materials.
- [ ] Rehearse presentation and record a practice run.
- [ ] Plan networking activities (schedule meetings, attend socials).
- [ ] Upload final slides and supplementary material to a public repository.
