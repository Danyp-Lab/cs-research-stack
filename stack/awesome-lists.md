## How to Discover More Awesome Lists

* **[Awesome Awesomeness](https://github.com/bayandin/awesome-awesomeness)** — The meta-list of awesome lists.
* **[Awesome Indexed](https://awesome-indexed.mathew-davies.co.uk/)** — Searchable index of all awesome lists.
* Search `awesome-<topic>` on GitHub to find curated lists for any technology or domain.
* **[Track Awesome List](https://www.trackawesomelist.com/)** — Weekly email digest of updates to awesome lists you follow.

---

> [!TIP]
> When you find a resource in an awesome list, always check the **last commit date** of the repository and the **date of last update** for the specific entry. Some lists go unmaintained; dead links and outdated tools are common.
